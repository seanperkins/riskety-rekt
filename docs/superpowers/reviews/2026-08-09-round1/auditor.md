The following are specification defects that would produce incorrect or non-reproducible ticks.

- **P0 — Orders can spend the same troops more than once.** [`attacks: [{from, to, count}]`]( /Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:134) only constrains each leg: “`count ≤ garrison(from) − 1`.” A garrison of 10 can submit two attacks of 9 from the same territory; each is valid individually, but resolution sends 18 troops while only 9 are expendable. Deploys and wagers have the same aggregate-reserve issue. Define per-faction, ordered/atomic resource validation: aggregate deployments plus wagers must not exceed reserve, and aggregate attacks per origin must not exceed `garrison - 1`; state whether an over-budget leg or the whole order is dropped.

- **P0 — The multiple-attacker rule is not executable.** The plan first says “sum all incoming attacks” ([line 162](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:162)), then says “every attacker takes losses against the defender” and “The largest surviving force takes the territory” ([lines 169–170](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:169)). For attacks of 8 and 6 into defense 10, the combined force wins with 4 survivors, but the spec provides no loss-allocation rule, therefore no way to determine which faction survives or owns the territory. Define a deterministic allocation algorithm, including ties, or make coalition attacks a single explicitly allocated force.

- **P0 — NO wagers have no snapshotted price.** `MarketAdapter` exposes only “`priceYes`” ([line 68](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:68)), while orders allow “`side`” YES or NO ([line 135](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:135)) and payout uses the chosen side’s `p` ([line 288](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:288)). With `priceYes = .80`, a 10-soldier NO stake pays either 13 using `.80` or 55 using `.20`; the plan cannot choose. Persist both side prices (or explicitly derive and snapshot `priceNo = 1 - priceYes`) and validate that the pending wager’s price corresponds to its side.

- **P0 — The published slate is neither durable nor available to resolution.** The plan requires “Prices are snapshotted once” ([line 317](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:317)) at 08:00 ([line 334](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:334)), but `Store` contains only `load/saveState` and `load/saveOrder` ([lines 77–82](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:77)). An order submitted at 18:00 has only `{marketId, side, stake}`; at tick time there is no persisted slate from which to validate membership or copy the frozen price into `pending`. Add a persisted, season/day-scoped slate and make escrow use it exclusively.

- **P1 — Settlement processing contradicts the rollover rule.** Step 1 says “**Settle yesterday’s wagers**” ([line 147](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:147)), but an unsettled wager “carries to the next tick” and is refunded only after 48 hours ([lines 356–357](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:356)). A day N−2 wager still unsettled at day N must be queried and potentially refunded, yet “yesterday’s” processing omits it. Define settlement over *all* pending wagers and an exact age boundary based on a stored close timestamp/instant.

- **P1 — Timing-bonus ties have no deterministic outcome.** The test list explicitly includes “two approvals sharing a timestamp” ([line 383](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:383)), but the rules award “the first player” and “the last approved action” ([lines 251–252](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:251)) without defining a tie-breaker. Two different players approved at the same timestamp can each plausibly receive Early Bird/Under the Wire, changing reserves and protection eligibility. Persist a stable approval/event ID and specify a deterministic ordering, or explicitly split/withhold tied bonuses.

- **P1 — One stated combat test case is unreachable under the specified rule.** The plan requests “an attack that wins with zero survivors” ([line 376](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:376)), while capture requires “`Attack > defense`” and survivors are “`attack − defense`” ([line 165](/Users/sean/sites/riskety-rekt/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-riskety-rekt-design.md:165)). With integer troop counts, a capture always leaves at least one survivor. Remove the impossible test or change combat semantics intentionally.
